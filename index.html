PctLens
A space is %20 in one place and + in another.
Open the app
The text a+b c becomes a%2Bb%20c with encodeURIComponent but a%2Bb+c as form data. Decode a+b as a form and you get a b.

What it does

  • Ten encoders on one input, with what each leaves unescaped
  • Per-character UTF-8 bytes and the sets that encode them
  • Decoder with the plus-sign, bad UTF-8, malformed and double-encoding flags
  • Sets taken from the WHATWG URL Standard
Follows the WHATWG URL Standard and RFC 3986. Static, client-side, nothing stored or sent. Source
