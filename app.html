PctLens

One string, ten encoders: RFC 3986, encodeURIComponent, encodeURI, form encoding and the WHATWG URL percent-encode sets. See what each one leaves alone, and decode with the plus-sign and double-encoding traps flagged.

Characters

Decode

Sets and the percent-decode algorithm follow the WHATWG URL Standard (fetched and read); the unreserved set follows RFC 3986; encodeURI and encodeURIComponent follow the behavior of Node and browsers (checked against Node on 1500 random strings). Text is always encoded as UTF-8; the legacy encodings the standard also describes (Shift_JIS and others) are not modelled. The path set here includes ^ as the fetched standard says; Node 22's own URL parser does not encode it yet, so a browser may differ on that one character. Lone surrogates are replaced with U+FFFD before encoding. Runs in your browser, nothing is sent anywhere.
